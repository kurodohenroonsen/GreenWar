How It Works

Buying is Voting

Every time we buy something, we tell a factory: "Keep going!". Every time we choose not to buy, we say: "Change your ways!". Factories listen very closely to this language.

You don't need to shout. Just choose the right basket. The giants will follow your lead!

Our Inventions

Three Inventions Helping Earth Heal

1. The Friendly Larvae That Eat Scraps

The black soldier fly has hungry baby larvae: they eat our vegetable peelings and leftovers in just three days. At the end, what remains is super-fertilizer for plants and healthy food for chickens and fish. Nothing is burned, nothing is wasted: it's a perfect loop!

2. The Digital Passport for Everyday Things

Imagine: every tomato, t-shirt, or toy has a tamper-proof passport. You scan it with a phone and see its whole journey: who made it, where, and whether everyone was treated fairly. If someone lies, it shows up immediately!

3. The Farm of the Future

A farm where everything is connected: leftovers feed larvae, larvae nourish the soil, soil grows healthy veggies, and sunshine powers everything. A farm with zero waste that leaves the land richer and more alive each year.
