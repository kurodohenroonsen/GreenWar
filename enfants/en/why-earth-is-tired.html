A True Story

Why is Earth Tired?

It isn't the fault of a single person. It is a story that started a long time ago, and one that we can change together.

80 Years Ago…

After a big war, everyone wanted to build more things, faster and faster.

Factories ran day and night. They made cars, toys, clothes, phones… Far more than anyone actually needed. To do that, we took trees, oil, metals, and water from nature without always putting them back.

Adults then invented a rule: "try not to damage too much". That's better than nothing. But making less damage is not the same thing as healing.

The Problem

The Three Giants and Their Three Desires

Three big groups decide what gets made. Each has an ambition that drives it, and none of them is "taking care of the Earth".

💰

Factories want money

They make things as cheaply as possible to sell as many as possible. If it's good for nature, great, but that isn't their main goal.

🏆

Big associations want renown

Some mostly want people to talk about them. They patch a small corner of the garden using money from the very companies that damaged it.

👑

Countries want power

They make laws, but often it's families who pay the bill with new taxes.

The giants aren't mean on purpose. They're just looking at their feet instead of looking at the garden. It's up to us to guide their steps!

The Journey of a Toy

Where does your favorite toy come from?

Look at the tag: it often comes from very far away. In some distant workshops, people work long hours for very little pay to produce mountains of toys. Sometimes, they are even children your age who cannot go to school because of this.

It's not your fault. But when nobody knows where things come from, nobody can stop it. That's why we must be able to trace the journey of every single object. Discover how on the next page!
