# Catalogue et Prompts des Styles Visuels Enfants — GREENWAR Junior

> **Document de référence artistique & pédagogique**  
> Ce document détaille les **3 univers graphiques** du site GREENWAR Junior (Enfants) et rassemble tous les prompts de génération des 12 illustrations phares.

---

## 1. Les 3 Univers Graphiques GREENWAR Junior

```
                           ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

                                    │                  │                  │
                                    ▼                  ▼                  ▼
                             Dessin animé 2D/3D  Documentaire réel   Animaux 3D Pixar
                             Mascotte Pépin      Éducation vivante   Inclusif universel
```

### Style 1 — Cartoon Coloré (Mascotte Pépin la petite pomme)
* **Description** : L'univers original illustré, chaleureux et ludique avec Pépin la petite pomme rouge souriante et son univers enchanteur.
* **Fichiers** : `01_pepin_mascotte.webp`, `02_terre_maison.webp`, etc.

### Style 2 — Réaliste / Documentaire Nature & Éducation
* **Description** : Photographies authentiques d'enfants explorateurs, de vraies pommes du verger, de vers de terre dans un compost pédagogique, et de jardins partagés en plein soleil. Idéal pour les écoles et institutions éducatives.
* **Fichiers** : `01_pepin_mascotte-real.webp`, `02_terre_maison-real.webp`, etc.

### Style 3 — Animation 3D Anthropomorphe (Style Zootopia / Pixar)
* **Description** : Univers 3D féerique avec une brigade d'animaux totémiques (renardeau à bonnet pomme, ourson Barnaby, castors bâtisseurs, faon, loutres scouts, chouettes gardiennes). Il élimine toutes les distinctions humaines (origine, genre, religion) au profit de l'amitié et de l'amour de la Terre.
* **Fichiers** : `01_pepin_mascotte-toon.webp`, `02_terre_maison-toon.webp`, etc.

## 3. Prompts Détaillés des 12 Illustrations

### 01. Pépin la Mascotte
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary close-up photograph of a beautiful crisp ripe organic red apple with two fresh green leaves resting on a rustic wooden farm table in a sunny orchard, natural golden morning light, authentic National Geographic photography, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation feature film style, Zootopia aesthetic: A super cute and cheerful anthropomorphic woodland creature with big expressive eyes, wearing a little red apple cap with green leaf ears, waving joyfully from a blooming sunny orchard, Pixar 3D CGI lighting, 8k.`

### 02. La Terre notre Maison
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph of planet Earth from orbit, showing bright blue oceans, vibrant green European and African continents with white swirling clouds, glowing atmosphere edge, realistic authentic NASA style photo, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation movie render, Zootopia Pixar style: A whimsical globe of Earth floating in starry space, covered with tiny charming animal treehouses, miniature windmills, crystal rivers, and cheerful little animal astronauts waving from the atmosphere, Pixar 3D lighting, 8k.`

### 03. Le Jardin Fatigué
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph of a school garden bed in summer: dry dusty ground with droopy sunflower leaves, children in gardening boots holding watering cans and wooden trowels, natural outdoor sunlight, authentic education photography, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animated feature film scene, Pixar Zootopia style: A friendly little bunny and baby hedgehog wearing garden aprons, preparing to water thirsty garden sunflowers with cute watering cans in a charming woodland backyard, sunny day, Pixar 3D CGI lighting, 8k.`

### 04. Les Trois Géants Polluants
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph with educational perspective: in the far background, three old industrial factory chimneys emitting steam; in the foreground, children with a teacher learning about clean solar panels and planting oak seedlings, natural outdoor lighting, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation movie render, Zootopia style: Three silly, giant clunky smokestack robots in the background looking surprised as cheerful animal kids (fox, bear, beaver) playfully plant solar flower windmills and green vines around them, Pixar CGI lighting, 8k.`

### 05. Le Voyage du Jouet Durable
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph in a warm sunny wooden workshop: children and an artisan grandfather happily repairing and painting durable handcrafted wooden toys and sailboats, natural sunlight, authentic candid moment, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation feature film style, Zootopia aesthetic: An elder badger craftsman in leather apron teaching a cheerful little beaver cub and fox kit how to craft and paint beautiful durable wooden toy boats in a cozy sunlit timber workshop, Pixar CGI lighting, 8k.`

### 06. La Terre qui se Soigne
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph of children joyfully exploring a vibrant restored wildflower meadow filled with blooming poppies, clover, and fluttering butterflies, warm golden hour sunlight, National Geographic nature photography, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation movie render, Zootopia style: Cheerful anthropomorphic animal kids (a cute deer fawn, a little bear cub, a bunny) joyfully running through a lush meadow of glowing magical wildflowers and colorful butterflies under a rainbow, Pixar CGI lighting, 8k.`

### 07. Les Quatre Équipes d'Amis
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph showing four collaborative community teams in a green village park: a family planting flowers, young scout volunteers cleaning a stream, teen woodworking apprentices building a birdhouse, and a friendly park ranger showing a forest map to kids, natural daylight, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation movie render, Zootopia style: Four cheerful animal squads working in harmony in a green town square: deer family planting saplings, otter scouts cleaning the stream, beaver carpenters building wooden shelters, and owl rangers checking nature maps, Pixar CGI lighting, 8k.`

### 08. Le Super-Pouvoir du Caddie
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph of a smiling young boy and girl with their mother in a local organic farm shop, holding a small wicker shopping basket filled with fresh crisp apples, carrots, and glass honey jars, warm natural store lighting, authentic photography, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `Pixar 3D animated character of an adorable cartoon bear cub pushing a wooden toy shopping cart with fresh garden fruits in a cozy village store, sunny lighting, 8k render.`

### 09. Les Super-Larves Recycleuses
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph of a science educator showing young curious school children a transparent organic vermicompost observation box with rich dark earth and beneficial compost worms, natural classroom daylight, authentic educational photography, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation movie render, Zootopia style: A friendly anthropomorphic beaver scientist and baby animals looking into a transparent wooden observation box where tiny glowing friendly cartoon larvae are happily turning fruit peels into rich sparkling black earth, Pixar CGI lighting, 8k.`

### 10. Le Passeport Magique
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph of an inquisitive girl scanning a digital QR code passport on a glass jar of organic wildflower honey using a tablet, revealing farm origins and flower photos on screen, soft natural kitchen lighting, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation movie render, Zootopia style: A clever little raccoon kid with round spectacles using a cute glowing wooden leaf scanner on an acorn jar, seeing friendly green holographic farm pictures appear in the air, Pixar CGI lighting, 8k.`

### 11. La Ferme du Futur
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph of an organic solar greenhouse farm: smiling children picking bright red ripe strawberries from rich dark living soil beds under soft diffused sunlight, natural candid agriculture photo, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animated movie render, Pixar Zootopia aesthetic: A cheerful little badger cub and fawn wearing garden hats picking ripe strawberries in a sunlit glass greenhouse with blooming plants, warm morning sunlight, 8k render.`

### 12. Le Club des Gardiens
* **Réaliste** : `Documentary photograph of a diverse group of happy smiling children in a sunlit forest clearing, proudly wearing wooden Earth Guardian badges on green ribbons and holding up eco-challenge certificates, natural golden hour lighting, authentic photography, 8k.`
* **3D Zootopia** : `3D animation movie render: A happy team of cute animal friends (bear, fox, rabbit) standing together smiling proudly in a sunny green meadow holding a green flag, Pixar style, sunny day, 8k.`
