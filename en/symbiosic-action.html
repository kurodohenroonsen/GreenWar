02 — Real-World Supply Chains

The symbiosic economy in practice

Three operational building blocks: complete biodegradation, cryptographic traceability, and the regenerative bio-agro-industrial hub.

Circular Biotechnology

Total biodegradation & sarcomusation

Les larves d'Hermetia illucens (mouche soldat noire) valorisent 100 % de la biomasse organique en 72 heures, sans incinération ni émission de méthane.

  • Absolute biosafety : respect strict de la règle d'or anti-prion européenne — zéro recyclage intra-espèce.
  • Certified Sterilization Méthode 1 : 133 °C, 3 bars, 20 minutes.
Decentralized Audit

Peer-to-peer cryptographic traceability

Every batch receives a digital passport d'un passeport biologique numérique infalsifiable, signé Ed25519 sur le réseau pair-à-pair AeterniTrak.

  1. Field Registration

    NFC badge and GPS tracking at every stage of the supply chain.

  2. Rapid Screening

    LFA / PCR screening for contaminants prior to transformation.

  3. Certified Sterilization

    Method 1: 133°C, 3 bars, 20 minutes.

  4. Immutable Signature

    The batch is cryptographically sealed; a single QR/NFC scan displays the full verifiable history in one second.

Closed-Loop Ecosystem

The regenerative bio-agro-industrial hub

An integrated facility uniting biodegradation, aeroponic greenhouses, micro-algae photobioreactors, and solar canopies.

Zero waste, massive carbon sequestration, and active regeneration of Earth's 510 million km².
